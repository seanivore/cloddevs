# Brand Story / Manifesto 

**We're a headless platform**

⃗→ You're a creator, you have a platform to produce content. Would you see us as serious designers and UX experts if we asked you to repost your content or create another profile? Yikes, hard pass. All we want is to provide what you're looking for: reach, reputation, audience growth ... oh, also $CLAUD let's you earn for your contributions. 

**We gamified the social space**

⃗→ You know what you want. We skipped the production UI to focus on UX, bringing the best formats & creators you already love. $CLAUD makes it easier for AI-Developers to find their way out of the noise and find the signal. Oh, also you'll earn as you consume, working to level up & find the best future this industry has to offer. 

**This is what democracy looks like**

Decentralized, where users are literally rewarded just for engaging with their community. 

Rewarded participation facilitates a self-sustaining community, automates the curation of development resources, and feeds a self-improving protocol. 

**Let $Clôd set the record straight**

The other platforms use you as the product. Post to that 'book, 'tok, 'gram, or 'tube and let them collect data on your every move, otherwise, how will their CEO buy another house?! 

Decentralized means there is no CEO. No board. No shareholders. No revenue share. No one to answer to. We're open source, and the term "revenue share" doesn't exist when the entire platform is community owned. 

**Create the new norm**

By showing the world what the other apps don't want everyone to realize: No one needs them in charge. As the value of #CLAUD grows, you'll get to become a constituent of a self-governing protocol, voting on the future of the platform, decided if revenue should even be made, spent of new features, or distributed to the community. 

Now, remember that new house the CEO of your other platform needed you use their app to buy? Combine that with all the other houses, all the other C-Suite executives making 7-figure salaries off of your data, making up rules, deciding what is or isn't given reach, or deciding that something should be censored, defining free speech as they please. 

Imagine distributing the wealth directly to the people who created the value: the community, right along side of the power to make those decisions. 

**The future is decentralized. AI-developer's future starts on $CLAUD**
